## Exact proved foundation scope

- GapSplitFloorStability proves the exact end-split floor-score identity
  and chooses a smaller positive parameter preserving every floor of a
  finite nonnegative gap list, with strict margins. This is arithmetic,
  not a full geometric inflation theorem.
- SixModelReflection constructs a symmetry of the existing necessary
  scalar ModelSix n. Full row gap lists are reversed, including both
  endpoint gaps, zero gaps and repeated values. Every one of its five
  non-anchor row defects is preserved under Fin.rev. It does not construct
  reflected planar coordinates or prove scalar realizability.
- N3SixModelTerminal transfers ModelSix 3 to the old N3Model at lam=1 and
  p=3+delta. It excludes defect 2 at rank 1, defect 2 at rank 3, and
  simultaneous endpoint-rank 0 and 4 defects 2. Rank 2 and arbitrary two
  unspecified rows are not asserted to be excluded by this adapter.
- ActualScorePreservingExpansion proves local projective-ray continuity,
  positive-scale sibling normalization, intercluster order and fixed-gap
  floor stability. Its positive legal-expansion witness shares a parameter
  with a supplied finite pert floor event. Identification of that pert
  family with all actual direction rows remains a separate task.
- FiniteFanScoreStability internally sorts any nonempty finite continuous
  coordinate family whose limiting values lie strictly in (0,pi). It
  intersects the finite permutation/gap events at one smaller parameter
  and proves eventual exact sorted-fan row-score stability. Repeated
  values and the singleton full-circle wrap remain present. It does not
  assert continuity of arbitrary cut representatives or a fixed label
  permutation near a collision.
