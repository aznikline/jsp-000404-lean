## Exact flat inflation

ActualFlatPotentialInflation strengthens the prior actual point-count
inflation: for every actual legal configuration of at least three points
and 0<tau<lambda, it gives tau<lambdaFinal<lambda, exactly the original
potential F distinct actual points, every final row score zero, and final
potential equal to F. No capacity or ordinary-point bound is assumed.

At an already-flat terminal configuration, finite actual gap floor margins
choose one strictly smaller scale while preserving every flat row. Actual
single-positive-row splits preserve original F and decrease F-cardinality.
This closes the previously omitted final-score-zero condition without
pretending that freely weakening an angle parameter preserves floors.
Independent sharp point-cardinality bounds in later strips remain open.

## Fixed actual deletion

ActualMinimumDeletionNormalForm writes deleted potential as the sum over
survivors of 2^oldScore times 2^actualGain. At a least-score point q, assume
an explicit cap divisible by 2^score(q), original potential over cap, and
this actual deletion at or below cap. The conclusions are exact:
original potential=cap+2^score(q), deleted potential=cap, every incoming
actual gain zero, and all retained actual row scores unchanged. The
power-of-two-cap adapter derives divisibility from the actual floor strip.
The outside-ray consequence uses the already proved common-cone progress.

These hypotheses are local data, not a proof that arbitrary over-capacity
configurations have that normal form. No global capacity is inferred.

## Closest pair and primitive scope

ClosestPairTriangleExclusion chooses a globally closest actual pair p,q.
The angle opposite that shortest side is at most pi/3. Under original
legality with lambda>pi/6, every triangle with base p,q and a third center x
contains no other distinct center in its closed convex hull. Collinear
boundary cases are excluded by actual legality, not by an assumed circular
ordering. Projection ties and repeated distance minima are allowed.

This gives no projective fan adjacency, merge theorem, bare 17 exclusion
or sharp cardinal bound. Those are separate remaining geometric steps.
