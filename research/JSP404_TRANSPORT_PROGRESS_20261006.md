## Actual bare exclusion to original weighted capacity

ActualCapacityFromCardinality.potential_le_of_bare_exclusion takes an
independent actual C+1-point angle exclusion above rho>=0. For any original
legal configuration at lambda>rho, it proves the original potential<=C.

above tau=(rho+lambda)/2>rho. Its injective C+1 prefix inherits actual
legality, contradicting the displayed bare exclusion.

The bare premise is explicit and must be proved independently. It is not

then follow at their original numeric thresholds. These conclusions do

## Actual common oriented cone

ConeDeletionProgress assumes all actual rays from q lie within angle
pi/2-lambda of one nonzero oriented vector u. Among the other actual points,
a minimum u-projection point sees q at angle at least lambda from every
other ray. Original legality supplies the complementary upper angles,
so this is genuine positive rowGain emission.

If q also has least original row score, its actual deletion preserves or
increases potential. Actual zero-incoming-gain and overfull-under-cap
normal forms consequently exclude that cone. Projection ties are allowed.
The theorem does not assert such a cone exists in every overfull
configuration and does not replace it with an unoriented projective gap.
A convex-hull exterior-sum or global cone-existence bridge remains open.
