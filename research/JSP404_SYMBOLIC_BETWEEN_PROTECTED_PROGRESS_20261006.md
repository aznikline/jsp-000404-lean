# Uniform between-profile exclusion and protected actual inflation

This publication is partial. The original arbitrary-N lower theorem remains
open for N >= 17 and still depends on `sorryAx`; this is not a complete prize
submission. Original `Statement.lean`, `MainTheorem.lean` and the attributed
foreign formula are unchanged.

## Uniform symbolic result

`SixExactFloorKernel.symbolic_between_impossible` proves, for every natural
n >= 4 and every HIGH `ModelSix n`, that row defects `(2,3,*,3,2)` are
impossible. The middle row is unrestricted. All ten triangle disjunctions
are split internally: the actual proof tree has 197 splits, 136 scoped
contradiction prefixes and 62 typed surviving directions. Each of the 1024
arms is covered exactly once. There is no caller mask, membership or
acyclicity hypothesis in the public theorem.

The independent audit checks every signature, actual source line, cycle
witness, source/receipt hash, saved command and normal artifact. Its 1560
hash checks and 132 producer receipts have no errors or missing strict
command evidence. All 346 symbolic source modules have genuine normal
build records; their 354 explicitly printed declarations are included in
the composed audit. The Interval command supplement is a fresh actual
strict check of its unchanged source. Existing PASS source, receipt and
stdout bytes were not rewritten.

The scope is this scalar profile. Other profiles, full later-strip
capacity and original arbitrary-N equality are not conclusions.

## Actual local consumers

`SmallConeLowCapacity` proves the original full LOW potential bound for
any legal actual configuration with actual n >= 3 whose outgoing ray pairs
at one anchor have angle at most beta < lambda/2. A saturated anchor is not
required. It also rules out saturation at rho = pi/(n+1/2) for an original
LOW counterexample by reusing the original physical rays. General NoSAT
capacity and existence of a suitable cone remain open.

`ActualSingleSplitScoreTable` and `ActualProtectedInflation` preserve any
injectively selected protected rows' positions and scores, and the full
original potential F. For 0 < tau < lambda, they construct exactly
p + (F - protectedMass) distinct actual points at a parameter strictly
between tau and lambda. Every final unprotected score is zero. Empty and
fully protected selections are included; no target capacity, initially
flat tail or desired point count is assumed.

`R2N4PureTailInflation` consumes actual n = 4 HIGH and three distinct
protected rows with scores 3,2,2. F = 21 gives a genuine `Fin 8` scene with
five zero-score tails; F = 22 gives `Fin 9` with six. Original protected
positions, scores and total potential remain exact. Actual reindexing
transports the dependent domains. No points are discarded and no bare
eight/nine-point exclusion follows.

`N4R2CriticalReifiedRow` retains all seven complete gaps, including both
ends, using actual permutation and scalar-sorting premises. Together with
`N4R2CriticalCycleSoundness`, it proves ordinary weak/strict affine-cycle
soundness and one conditional five-arc actual sample. The full 24,698-word
packet traversal remains diagnostic evidence, rather than a kernelized
finite-capacity theorem.

`SharpNearestGeometry` contributes two actual metric lemmas. A ray angle
at p below lambda/2 gives a strictly nearer third point at q; two weak
nearest-to-a points with angle at a below pi/3 coincide. This does not
supply three-run extraction, debt-count closure or a full critical-model
exclusion.
