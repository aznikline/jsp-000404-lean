## Genuine weighted minimum-cardinality model

ActualMinimumCardBandMaximizer chooses the fewest actual points among
configurations realizing the already attained band maximum M. The choice
ranges over the same parameter band and the same potential M. It does not
assume a sharp cardinality bound, a desired removable vertex, or a flat
realization. Its weighted model retains actual points and original scores.

Every actual deletion leaving at least three points has potential strictly
less than M. Otherwise band maximality and minimal cardinality would give
a smaller realization of M. This is a strict bound against M, not a proof
that the deletion lies below the sharp capacity.

The three sufficient removable-point certificates are consequently excluded.
Every positive actual gain moves to a strictly higher original score, so the
actual emission graph is acyclic. The module also proves a genuine original
LOW counterexample supplies such a model, its rows are nonsaturated, and a
strictly smaller scale in the same open band still realizes the model.
No cardinality <=n+1, convex-position conclusion or geometric dichotomy is
assumed or proved.

## Local closest-pair constraint

ClosestPairZeroGainWitness chooses a genuine globally closest pair and
orients it from the endpoint with higher score to the endpoint with lower
score, with ties allowed. The excluded CertA supplies zero actual gain in
that direction. An actual third point j then satisfies

- lam/2 <= angle(q,p,j) < lam;
- pi-2*lam < angle(p,q,j) <= pi-lam;
- angle(p,j,q) <= angle(q,p,j).

For lam<=pi/4, inner(cq-cp,cj-cq)>0: j lies strictly beyond q's perpendicular
hyperplane in the direction from p to q. This local witness does not prove
projective adjacency, an inverse split, a legal merge or a sharp point bound.
