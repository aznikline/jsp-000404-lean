## Attained actual maximum, independent coarse bound

ActualMaximumMassReduction first proves the classical coarse point and
potential power bound independently. Within a given parameter band, the
possible actual flat cardinalities are bounded natural numbers. A supplied
original counterexample gives a nonempty actual member; selecting the
largest realized cardinality therefore produces a genuine actual maximum.
No attained real angle optimum, sharp capacity or free maximum is assumed.

LOW gives an actual flat maximum M with 2^n<M<=2^(n+1), within the original
LOW band. HIGH gives sendovCap n true<M<=2^(n+1), within
(pi/(n+1),pi/(n+1/2)]. The realized configuration is actual/legal, every
score is zero, and its potential equals M. For n=4, LOW counter-models
would have 17..32 points and HIGH 21..32; these are reductions, not exclusions.

Any actual deletion remains legal in that band. If its potential exceeded
M, exact actual flat inflation would construct a larger realized flat
cardinality in the same band, contradicting maximality. Thus its deletion
potential is at most M. FlatDeletionBudget translates this actual ledger
into gain<=1 for every survivor and at most one positive incoming emitter.
The LOW and HIGH functional consumers derive the maximum and all deletion
bounds internally from the original counterexample. The caller supplies
neither maximality nor a desired deletion cap.

These results do not rule out the resulting real counter-models. Global
sharp capacity, bare 17/21 and general all-N remain open.
